Who I am

I'm a second-year Computer Engineering student at AISSMS Institute of Information Technology, Pune, with a habit of picking a problem and building until it works — whether that means soldering a battery pack or debugging a React component at 1am.

My biggest project so far is PCAP (Portable Clean Air Purifier) — a wearable HEPA H13 air purifier my team designed and built from scratch, which turned into a published research paper. I'm currently splitting my time between DSA practice in C++ and AI/ML, getting ready for placement season.

I care about systems that are simple enough to actually use every day — that's as true for my code as it is for the habit trackers I build for myself.

Selected work

PCAP — Portable Clean Air Purifier

200 · Published

A wearable HEPA H13 air purifier with a 3D-printed PETG enclosure, an 18650 battery pack, and a centrifugal blower — built as a multidisciplinary capstone with a six-member team, aligned with UN SDG 3. The project was written up as a full research paper and published in IJRPR, alongside a symposium abstract and documented cost-savings analysis.

Role: Team member — design, build & documentationOutput: Published IJRPR paper, working prototype
